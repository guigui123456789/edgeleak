FAQ

Common questions.

Which poker platforms are supported?+
Currently PokerStars is fully supported for hand analysis. Winamax support is coming soon. The Range Explorer, EV Calculator and Stats Analyzer work independently of any platform.
How accurate are the GTO ranges?+
Ranges are generated by AI trained on GTO solver data. They are accurate for standard spots and excellent for learning — ideal for players up to NL500.
Can I cancel my Pro plan anytime?+
Yes, cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal. Your Pro access remains active until the end of the billing period.
Is my hand history data private?+
Yes. Your hands are only used to generate your analysis in real-time and are never stored or shared with third parties.
Do I need a credit card to sign up?+
No. The free plan requires only an email address. A card is only needed when you choose to upgrade to Pro.
