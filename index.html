FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Is EdgeLeak allowed by major poker sites?+
Yes. EdgeLeak is a study tool — it does not run during play and does not provide real-time assistance. That keeps it compliant with GG, Stars, WPT and others.
How accurate are the ranges?+
Ranges are derived from standard solver outputs (PioSolver, GTO Wizard equivalents) for the most studied spots in MTT and cash. They are accurate for spots up to NL500 and high-stakes MTT.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. One click in your billing portal. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing cycle, then drops back to Free. Your saved custom ranges remain accessible.
Where does my data live?+
Your account and saved ranges are stored on Supabase (EU region). Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not sell your data, ever. You can export and delete it from Settings.
Do I need to know solver theory to benefit?+
No. The legend explains every color, hover gives you exact frequencies, and tooltips break down the strategic intuition for each range. You learn by browsing.
